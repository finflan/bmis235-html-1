A page about our Braden Huff

Braden Huff is a 6-foot-10 forward who wears No. 34 for the Gonzaga men’s basketball team. Huff is a four-star recruit from Glenbard West High School in Glen Ellyn, Illinois, who signed with Gonzaga in 2021.

Off the court, Braden Huff is a Business Administration major at Gonzaga with a concentration in marketing. His work in the classroom has earned him a spot on the West Coast Conference All-Academic Team two years in a row. He was first named in 2025 with a 3.48 GPA, and he earned the honor again in 2026 with a 3.37 GPA. Balancing a demanding business degree with the schedule of a top-ranked Division I basketball program shows he is committed to being a strong student as well as a standout player.

Huff set a career high of 37 points on 16-for-18 shooting in a win over Campbell.

Three facts about Huff

  • Braden Huff was born on August 28, 2003, and is listed at about 250 pounds.
  • Huff committed to Gonzaga in September 2021, choosing the Zags over Virginia Tech after a campus visit.
  • Huff scored 18 points in just his second start of the season to help Gonzaga beat Saint Mary's 58-51 in the West Coast Conference Tournament championship game.
